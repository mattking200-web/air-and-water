🔄 E2.3: The Water Cycle

Water goes on an amazing journey called the water cycle! It travels from the ground to the sky and back again, over and over!

The Four Stages

The water cycle has four main stages: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and collection. Let's learn about each one!

☀️

1. Evaporation

Sun heats water, turns it to vapor!

☁️

2. Condensation

Water vapor makes clouds!

🌧️

3. Precipitation

Water falls as rain or snow!

🌊

4. Collection

Water gathers in lakes and rivers!

How It Works

Evaporation: The sun warms up water in oceans, lakes, and rivers. The water turns into water vapor (a gas) and floats up into the air. You can't see water vapor!

Condensation: High in the sky, the water vapor gets cold. It turns back into tiny water droplets. These droplets stick together and form clouds!

Precipitation: When the water droplets in clouds get too heavy, they fall back to Earth! This is rain. In winter, the water can freeze and fall as snow or hail!

Collection: The water that falls collects in rivers, lakes, and oceans. Some soaks into the ground. Then the sun heats it up again and the cycle starts over!

The Cycle Never Stops!

The water cycle has been happening for millions of years! The water you drink today is the same water that dinosaurs drank! It just keeps moving around and around!

🧊 E2.4: States of Water

Water is amazing because it can be three different things! It can be solid ice, liquid water, or gas (steam). Temperature makes water change!

🧊

How Temperature Changes Water

Temperature is very important! When water gets cold, it freezes into ice. When ice gets warm, it melts into liquid water. When liquid water gets very hot, it evaporates into steam!

❄️

Freezing

0°C or 32°F - Water becomes ice!

🌡️

Melting

Above 0°C - Ice becomes water!

🔥

Boiling

100°C or 212°F - Water becomes steam!

💧

Condensing

Steam cools - Becomes water!

Water States in Nature

We see all three states of water in the environment! Ice is in glaciers and snowflakes. Liquid water is in rivers and rain. Water vapor is in clouds and steam from hot springs!

My Water-Saving Plan

Let's make a plan to save water! Here are some things you can do every day:

  • Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth
  • Take shorter showers (under 5 minutes!)

  • Tell an adult if you see a leaky faucet
  • Reuse water when you can (like watering plants with leftover water)
  • Only flush the toilet when needed
